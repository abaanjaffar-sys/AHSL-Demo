What the result means

The six report statuses

Every check returns one of these. Amendments never overwrite a report: a corrected report gets a new revision with its own fingerprint and QR code, and the old revision shows as Amended.

How it works

From approval to verification

1

Approved with MFA

Routine reports go to normal approval; flagged reports go to expert review. Either way, the Head of Department or Lab Manager approves with multi-factor authentication. The AI never approves.

2

Fingerprinted and signed

The report's key data goes through SHA-256 to make a unique fingerprint, which is signed with AHSL's private key, locked in a hardware security module. The QR code carries the signature and the report reference.

3

Checked on the portal

The portal checks the signature with AHSL's public key, recalculates the fingerprint, compares it with the stored record and looks up the current status.

Where the records live

Until mid-2029

Report registry (standard database)

Fingerprint, status, revision and signature reference for every report. Used in this prototype.

Always in LIMS

Full report and confidential data

Test results and client data stay inside AHSL's LIMS. Only the fingerprint and a few basic details are stored for verification.

From mid-2029

Permissioned blockchain

Nothing can be deleted and every change is added as a new entry. Past records move onto it and QR codes stay exactly the same.
